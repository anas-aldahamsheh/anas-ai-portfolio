# Job Fit Analyzer

This replaces the unclear "AI Project Matcher" name.

## Purpose
A recruiter pastes a job description. The system maps each meaningful requirement to verified portfolio evidence.

## Output structure
- requirement;
- evidence status: Supported / Partially supported / Not found;
- supporting project/experience citations;
- short explanation;
- uncertainty note when relevant.

## Prohibited behavior
- do not fabricate a skill;
- do not infer years of experience not documented;
- do not claim certification not present;
- do not convert "not found" into a negative personal judgment;
- do not silently use web information about the owner.

## Privacy
Job descriptions are not persisted by default.
