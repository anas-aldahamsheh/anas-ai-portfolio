# Product Vision

The portfolio should make a recruiter understand the owner's work quickly while allowing an engineer to inspect technical depth.

## Core experience layers

### Layer 1 — Scan
Within seconds, a visitor should understand:
- professional focus;
- strongest work categories;
- major projects;
- how to open CV;
- how to ask the portfolio AI.

### Layer 2 — Explore
Visitors can:
- inspect projects;
- read project deep dives;
- filter work;
- inspect architecture and evidence;
- explore AI evaluation demonstrations.

### Layer 3 — Ask
Visitors can ask natural-language questions rather than hunting through pages.

### Layer 4 — Verify
Answers cite portfolio sources and project records.

### Layer 5 — Match
A recruiter can paste a job description into Job Fit Analyzer and view evidence mapping.

## Design personality

Quiet confidence:
- no noisy sales language;
- no unsupported superlatives;
- no fake social proof;
- no fake counters;
- no decorative "AI magic";
- evidence before claims.
